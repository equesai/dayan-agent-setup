# Dayan Agent Setup

The installer that connects an AI agent on your computer to
[Dayan](https://dayan.equesai.tech), so Dayan can study and build with you
right on your own machine.

## Download

**Windows 10 / 11:** [DayanSetup.exe](https://github.com/equesai/dayan-agent-setup/releases/latest/download/DayanSetup.exe)

**macOS and Linux:** coming here with the next update. Until then, sign in to
Dayan and use **Quick › Run Dayan Agent on my device**.

## Your key

The setup asks for your Dayan key. Get it on any device where you are signed
in to Dayan, your phone included: open
[dayan.equesai.tech/agent-key](https://dayan.equesai.tech/agent-key), choose
**Generate a new key**, and paste it into the setup.

## Good to know

- Windows may say it protected your PC, because the file is new to it: choose
  **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Each release lists the SHA-256 of its files in `SHA256SUMS`.
